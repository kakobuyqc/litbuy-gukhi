The most useful product list explains why an item is worth comparing. Keep your process simple, specific and easy to revisit.

Start your research ↗
  1. 01

    Choose one category at a time

    Begin with shoes, a hoodie or one accessory. A focused list makes similar items easier to compare.

  2. 02

    Save the exact listing and variant

    Record the URL, selected color, size, displayed currency and date checked. Mark missing details as unknown.

  3. 03

    Check fit and construction

    Compare measurements with an item you own. Read material descriptions and look for clear product photographs.

  4. 04

    Confirm costs before committing

    Keep the listed price separate from shipping, service fees and any applicable import charges. Confirm live terms with the relevant service.

What belongs in each row?

Use these fields to make a fashion comparison worksheet more useful. A descriptive note is better than an unsupported rating.

Suggested fields for a product-research spreadsheet
FIELD WHAT TO RECORD WHY IT HELPS
Product reference Listing title, category, URL and date checked. Lets you return to the correct item and identify older notes.
Selected variant Color, size and any available model or style reference. Keeps your comparison tied to the version you actually want.
Fit information Relevant measurements, units and size-chart notes. Helps compare fit beyond a simple S, M or L label.
Material details Stated composition, care instructions and missing information. Separates documented details from visual assumptions.
Cost breakdown Item price, currency and separately confirmed extra costs. Prevents an item-only price from becoming a misleading total.
Open questions Unconfirmed availability, seller terms or service compatibility. Shows what still needs checking before an order.

A few practical answers for anyone building a Litbuy shopping checklist.

What is a Litbuy spreadsheet?

In this guide, a Litbuy spreadsheet means a product-research worksheet used to organize shopping links, categories, prices, sizing notes and comparison details. This page is an independent guide, not an official Litbuy service or a downloadable product database.

How do I start browsing fashion finds?

Choose a category near the top of this page to open CAFANS. Review the individual listing, record its URL and available details, and compare it with alternatives before making a decision.

What should I check before saving a product link?

Check the selected variant, size chart, materials, product photographs, seller information, listed price and date checked. Record missing information as unknown instead of filling it with assumptions.

Does a spreadsheet confirm authenticity or quality?

No. A spreadsheet organizes information but does not authenticate merchandise or guarantee quality. Brand imagery, listing titles and product links are not proof of authorization or authenticity.

Can I use the same size across every listing?

Do not assume that the same size label means the same measurements. Compare the specific listing's size chart with an item you already own, and check how the seller measures each dimension.

Are delivery costs and agent compatibility verified here?

No. This guide does not verify live delivery prices, availability or whether a listing is supported by Litbuy. Confirm those details with the relevant seller or service before ordering.
